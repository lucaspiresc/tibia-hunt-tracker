# Inventory reference

`inventory-reference.jpg` is a screenshot of the inventory illustration in
CipSoft's official Tibia manual, section 3.4.1, captured on 2026-09-22.

Page: https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?subtopic=manual&section=interface#inventory

Image displayed by that page: https://static.tibia.com/images/gameguides/inventory.jpg

Artwork belongs to CipSoft GmbH. Included as a small visual recognition reference,
not as an assertion of affiliation, approval, or permission to automate gameplay.
The screenshot has JPEG artifacts. Coordinates in equipment.py refer to this
115 × 163 pixel reference, not the user's desktop. The matcher ignores item
interiors, soul/capacity values and conditions when locating the inventory.

The illustration shows the normal gray inventory. Other client appearances,
including the golden Adventurer's Blessing frame, are not validated. Tests reuse
this reference with synthetic items/transforms; they do not substitute for
independent screenshots or a Windows/Tibia live test.
